About Me

I'm a BSIT student from the Technological Institute of the Philippines with a passion for creating digital experiences. I specialize in 3D modeling, web development, and Figma UI/UX design, combining creativity and technical skills to bring ideas to life.

I work with tools such as Blender, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, C++, and Oracle Forms for database-driven applications.
